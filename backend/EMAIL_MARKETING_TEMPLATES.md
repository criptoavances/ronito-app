# Ronito Email Marketing Sequences

Email marketing sequence designed for new user onboarding. Generated by AI campaign design agent.

**Series:** Welcome Sequence  
**Target:** New users (day 0-7)  
**Goal:** Drive completion of onboarding and establish daily habit

---

## Setup Instructions for Systeme.io

1. **Create Templates:**
   - Log into Systeme.io
   - Go to Email Sequences / Templates
   - Create 4 new email templates with IDs: `welcome_email`, `big_goal_reminder`, `week_checkin`, `week_one_recap`
   - Copy-paste HTML content from above

2. **Configure Variables:**
   - Enable these variables in each template:
     - `{{first_name}}`
     - `{{big_goal}}` (Email 2 only)
     - `{{verification_link}}` (Email 1 only)
     - `{{app_link}}` (all except Email 1)

3. **Create Sequence:**
   - Create automation: "Welcome Sequence"
   - Trigger: New subscriber signup
   - Add emails in order:
     - Email 1 (immediate)
     - Email 2 (24 hours later)
     - Email 3 (72 hours later)
     - Email 4 (7 days later)

4. **API Integration:**
   - Get your Systeme.io API key
   - Set environment variable: `SYSTEME_IO_API_KEY=<your_key>`
   - Backend will automatically enroll users via `/api/marketing/enroll`

## Personalization Data Needed at Signup

**Required fields to capture:**
- `first_name` - For personalization
- `big_goal` - User's "WHY" statement (can be voice or text)
- `email` - For verification and marketing

**Implementation:**
- Backend stores these in `users` table
- When users sign up, they're auto-enrolled in `email_sequences` table
- Systeme.io pulls these variables when sending each email

---

## Compliance Notes

- All emails include unsubscribe option (Systeme.io adds automatically)
- Verification email required before marketing emails sent
- Complies with CAN-SPAM and GDPR (user explicitly signed up)
- No bait-and-switch: all emails deliver value, not just sales pitches

---

## Performance & Analytics

Track these metrics in Systeme.io:
- **Email 1:** Verification click rate (should be 40-60%)
- **Email 2:** Onboarding start rate (goal: 30%+ of openers)
- **Email 3:** Reply rate (indicates engagement level)
- **Email 4:** Voice feature exploration (conversion metric)

Adjust timing or copy based on what's working.
